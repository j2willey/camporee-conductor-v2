Live demo · version 2

One real camporee.
Three ways in.

Design a station-based competition, score it on volunteers' phones, and roll the scores up into standings, awards and the announcer's script. This demo runs a real design: the 2026 Circus camporee, with 33 activities.

New and improved

Rebuilt from the ground up in TypeScript. Version 2 is a complete rewrite, designed to be easier to maintain and extend: one scoring engine behind every screen, a judge page that keeps working offline, and event designs saved as portable documents.

The demo is shared and may be reset at any time. Please don't enter real names or contacts.
