# ADR-0007 — Divisions: official-assigned, changeable, director-configured awards
Date: 2026-10-25 · Status: Accepted
**Decision:**
- An Official assigns each entrant's Division at registration; it can be changed later (scores stay with the entrant, rankings recompute). No ages collected.
- Placements/ribbons are per Division only.
- Overall awards (Top Patrol, Top Dog): the director chooses per Event — per Division or event-wide.
- Which tiers can be divided (Teams only, or Groups too) is the director's choice per Event/Competition.
**Why:** real case — 40 patrols, 10 older-scout patrols compete among themselves, 30 younger among themselves, same Challenges. Flexibility over hard-coded rules because other events will differ.
